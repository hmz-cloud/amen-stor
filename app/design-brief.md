# LOOMERE design brief

Design read: A premium streetwear storefront for design-conscious shoppers who want restrained garments with a point of view.

Concept spine: The private showroom. The homepage behaves like a dark gallery where each garment is introduced as an object, then the collection opens into a clean buying surface.

Delivery tier: cinema.

Animation mode: animated-website

Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous film keeps the first impression focused and avoids a stitched-together campaign feel.

Journey: Establish the person and silhouette. Move closer to the fabric and hardware. Resolve on the full collection and the LOOMERE point of view.

World grammar: graphite studio, bone typography, restrained cobalt accent, hard side light, tactile cotton and brushed metal, slow forward camera movement, no on-screen text in the footage.

Mobile framing: keep the face, torso, and garment silhouette in the center-safe area; use the same quiet crop on narrow screens.

Palette: #11110f ink, #e9e6df bone, #b9b6ae ash, #3557d4 cobalt. Cobalt is used only for interactive emphasis and links.

Type: condensed sans display for headlines, neutral grotesk for body, wide letter spacing for utility labels.

Dial values: DESIGN_VARIANCE 8, MOTION_INTENSITY 6, VISUAL_DENSITY 3.

Section plan: cinematic journey, category rail, asymmetric collection grid, brand statement, newsletter footer.

Asset plan: one local hero film generated from the reference hero still, exact first-frame poster, two supporting campaign images, two product images, and a compact icon-free navigation.

CTA inventory: SHOP THE COLLECTION uses a cobalt arrow treatment; VIEW DETAILS uses a low-contrast text underline; JOIN THE LIST uses a boxed submit control.
